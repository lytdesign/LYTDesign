## About

I build software that is simple, accessible, and calm to use. Modern software has become complex, distracting, and data driven. LYTDesign is my attempt to build the opposite: thoughtful design, privacy, and simplicity that help people do more with less friction.

- **Simplified.** Most software adds. I start from what can be taken away.
- **Human.** I design for a real person in a real moment, not for engagement metrics.
- **Accessible.** Accessibility is a starting constraint, not a final pass.

<details>
<summary><b>Why calm software, privacy, and thoughtful interfaces matter</b></summary>

<br>

**Calm software.** Constant notifications, infinite feeds, and attention driven design make much of modern software exhausting, even when it works as intended. Calm software informs without demanding, and lets a person finish what they came to do and then leave.

**Privacy is foundational, not a feature.** Once a business model depends on collecting user data, every future design decision bends toward extracting more of it. I avoid that dependency entirely, because privacy that can be turned off or sold later was never really privacy.

**Thoughtful interfaces.** An interface is the whole relationship between a person and a piece of software. I treat every interaction as something worth shaping deliberately.

</details>

## Foundation

Commitments every LYTDesign product is expected to hold to:

- **Simplicity:** no clutter, no noise, only focus
- **Human centred:** every product starts from real usability, not engagement targets
- **Accessibility:** built into the design from the first line, not added afterward
- **Privacy:** no ads, no tracking, no data resale; revenue comes from features, never from user data
- **Long term thinking:** intention and lasting value before trends

---

## LYTGrid™

LYTGrid is my design system: a box based, cognitive UI approach where every feature gets its own visible space, and nothing is hidden behind menus, tabs, or deep navigation. It reduces the mental effort needed just to operate an app, so a pattern learned once is understood everywhere.

Its most developed implementation today lives inside QuraLYT. The intent is to reuse it across future LYTDesign products.

## A Personal Note

LYTDesign is an independent product studio, founded in 2025 and built by one person rather than a company or a team. I didn't start it to build any single product. I started it because I kept encountering software that felt like it was working against the people using it, and I wanted to find out what the opposite would look like.

I am not asking the world to download what I build. I am asking people to build with me, because the world does not need more software. It needs softer software.

*S.M., Founder, LYTDesign™*
